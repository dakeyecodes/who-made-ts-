Decompilation reconstructs readable code from a finished program. For old games, that can make new ports and ambitious modifications possible.

Developer Chris Lewis finished the later project in roughly one-seventh of the elapsed time. AI coding agents—tools that repeatedly write, compile and check code—were part of the change, alongside experience and community expertise.

ⓘ what these numbers measure

These are two different projects, not a controlled speed test. Lewis measures the sequel from first commit to final matching function; 84 days is his project-level count for the original game. Both involved AI, and neither was a solo effort. These figures do not establish a universal “years to hours” conversion.

The Modern Frankensteins Have No Victors

When AI writes a mod’s code, who gets called its maker?

This investigation follows two projects by chasmlol: SkyCraft, which connects Minecraft and Skyrim, and a Modern Warfare 2 mashup that adds skating and a block-built world.

Their public development histories name contributors and include tags naming Claude, an AI coding tool, as a co-author. What should those names mean in the credits?

You’ll follow the work behind these mods, look at a few code excerpts and decide how you would credit the people involved.

The tree contains 226 catalogue entries: 144 projects with AI involvement disclosed, 81 unverified projects and one configuration-only record. Select any node to open its project details and inspect its record. Purple nodes show projects with AI involvement disclosed. The six cards below retain their separate commit counts. Checked 7 Nov 2026 using GitHub's commits API for the default branch, with up to 100 commits per response. OWCraft, ValCraft and Killcraft returned 3, 31 and 17 commits respectively—each below the page limit. Counts match the case-insensitive text “Co-Authored-By: Claude” in commit messages. Each card links to a fixed inspected HEAD. These are disclosed co-authorship counts, not percentages of AI-written lines, labor, or original work. Untagged commits remain unknown.
